# SaathiCare — Privacy & Healthcare Disclaimer

**Version:** 1.0.0 (Demonstration Prototype)  
**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

---

## 1. Demonstration Prototype Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SaathiCare is a demonstration prototype.**  
> Data is stored locally in your browser's `LocalStorage` and is not synchronised across users, devices, or cloud servers.  
> **Do not enter real personal health information (PHI), sensitive medical history, or real contact details.**  
> SaathiCare does not provide medical diagnosis, dosage calculations, or clinical advice, and does not replace consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

---

## 2. Data Storage & Telemetry

- **No Server Tracking:** SaathiCare does not transmit user data to external servers, telemetry networks, or cloud databases.
- **LocalStorage Storage Key:** Application data resides solely on your client device under `saathicare_app_data_v1`.
- **Data Erasure:** You can completely clear all stored data at any time by:
  1. Clicking **"Reset All Demo Data"** in the Settings tab.
  2. Clearing your browser's site data/cookies for this domain.

---

## 3. Simulated Features

The following capabilities are simulated for demonstration purposes:
- **Demo Role Switching:** Switching between Meera, Aarav, and Priya simulates multi-user perspectives on a single device without server-side authentication.
- **Care Circle 6-Digit Invitation Codes:** Code validation and joining occur entirely within the local browser sandbox.
- **Notification Escalation:** Dose alerts are generated locally in the browser feed; no real SMS or email messages are dispatched.
